# Executive Career Hub V16

## What changed

- **Evidence fit** is calculated only from the master resume and verified evidence.
- **Tailored ATS coverage** is calculated after generation against the full final resume, including older experience, tools, education, and languages.
- Benefits and employer perks are excluded from keyword scoring.
- Semantic requirement groups replace fragile exact-word counting.
- Unsupported critical claims such as MDF, QBR, Israel, Hebrew localization, Smartsheet, cloud-provider, reseller, and IT-channel experience are automatically removed unless present in the evidence.
- Removed bullets are replaced by verified, JD-ranked achievements.
- The interface shows matched requirements, transferable coverage, real gaps, and claims removed by the validator.

No Supabase schema changes are required.
